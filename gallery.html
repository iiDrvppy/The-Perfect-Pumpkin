Did You Know?

Pumpkins are a type of winter squash. They come in different shapes, sizes, and colors.

Three Cool Pumpkin Facts

  1. Pumpkins are made mostly of water.
  2. Pumpkin seeds can be roasted and eaten.
  3. Pumpkins can be used in pies, soups, and other foods.

How Big Can Pumpkins Get?

Some pumpkins grow bigger than the ones we see at the store. Giant pumpkins can weigh hundreds or even thousands of pounds!

Why Pumpkins Are Popular

Pumpkins are a Halloween favorite because people use them for decorations, carving, and food. They are a big part of Halloween!
