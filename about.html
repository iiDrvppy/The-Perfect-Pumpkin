How to Pick the Perfect Pumpkin

Tips for finding a good pumpkin

Home How to Pick a Pumpkin Pumpkin Facts

1. Check the Color

Look for a pumpkin with a bright orange color. Orange is a popular choice for Halloween.

2. Check if it's Firm

A good pumpkin should feel hard. Avoid pumpkins with soft spots or damaged areas.

3. Look at the Stem

Choose a pumpkin with a hard stem. Do not lift a pumpkin by the stem because it could break off.

4. Choose the Right Size

Large pumpkins are great for carving, and small pumpkins work well as decorations.

Quick Pumpkin Checklist

  • Bright color
  • Hard surface
  • Attached stem
  • Right size for your plans

The perfect pumpkin is the one that works best for you
